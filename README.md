# Road Rush

A mobile-friendly, one-page racing game. Open the site on a phone or computer and play in the browser — no app or downloads.

**Play:** [https://raringdude.github.io/road-rush/](https://raringdude.github.io/road-rush/)

Steer around traffic on a night highway, build speed, and chase a high score. Your best score is saved on the device.

## How to play

Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer. On a phone you can also tilt. On a computer use the arrow keys or A and D. Avoid other cars and the edge of the road. Near misses add bonus points. Tap Restart after a crash.
